# Bathroom Recipes

These recipes cover the automations in our bathroom. An mmWave presence sensor
covers the room, with separate zones for the shower and the toilet, and — along
with the time of day, the sun and the weather — drives each of the devices below
independently:

- [**Blind**](blind.md): the window blind, opened and closed by time of day and
    eased open gradually on bright mornings.
- [**Extractor fan**](fan.md): the extractor fan, run while the shower or toilet
    is in use and switched off once the room has been vacant for a while.
- [**Lights**](lights.md): the main lights, dimmed to suit the time of day and
    dropped right down at night when the adjoining bedroom is in bed mode.
- [**Power Shower**](power-shower.md): the water pump's high-pressure mode,
    turned on while someone showers and released afterwards through the garden
    irrigation.
- [**Towel rack**](towel-rack.md): the heated towel rack, run for a couple of
    hours after a shower to dry the towels.
